YOUR WORKFLOW

From your deck to your speaking notes

01

Bring your PowerPoint

Sign in and choose a presentation up to {{ max_upload_mb }} MB that you are authorized to process.

Keep any visuals you want analyzed embedded in the file.

02

Set your audience and language

Choose who you are presenting to and the language for your speaker notes so the explanation suits the people in the room.

03

Choose depth and note length

Set the level of explanation and the amount of text you need per slide, then add any relevant context or instructions.

04

Choose how slides are analyzed

Use Text-only for slide text, or Text + Vision for charts, diagrams and images when available on your plan.

Check how existing notes will be handled before starting.

05

Generate and review the draft

Start processing and follow the progress in the workspace, then review any warnings and check the generated explanation against your slides.

06

Refine and rehearse

Open the generated PowerPoint copy, check names and figures, adapt the notes to your own voice and rehearse aloud.

Your original PowerPoint is not overwritten.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Help with your presentation

My slide contains an image but little text+

Use Text + Vision when available on your plan and embed the image in the PowerPoint so its content can be analyzed.

A file or slide could not be processed+

Read the message in the workspace, check that the file opens in PowerPoint and review the file-size limit; contact Support with the job reference if you need help.

How should I review AI-generated notes?+

Compare the explanation with your slide, verify facts and figures and remove any unsupported claims before you present.

Can I continue after a free preview?+

Follow the upgrade option for your existing presentation; processing the remaining slides requires an active Pro plan and enough available credits.
