## Product
NotePreso helps users generate AI speaker notes
for PowerPoint presentations.

## Subscription
NotePreso Pro: USD 10 per month.
Pro includes 1,000 monthly AI Slide Credits.
Credits refresh according to the account billing cycle.

## Credit Top-ups
250 credits: USD 4.
500 credits: USD 7.
750 credits: USD 10.

Prices exclude any applicable additional taxes.

Top-up Credits persist across monthly resets.
Pro Credits are consumed before Top-up Credits.
Both contribute to the total available credit balance.

## Payment
Paddle processes NotePreso payments.
The Live top-up flow has been tested successfully.
Purchased credits are granted after confirmed payment.

Recurring subscription renewal requires further testing.

## Support
Support email: billing@actiacarbon.com

For billing, refund, or missing-credit issues,
contact support with the transaction reference,
account email, and a description of the issue.

Do not send passwords, API keys, CVV,
or full payment card details.
