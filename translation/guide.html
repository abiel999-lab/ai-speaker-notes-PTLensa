How to translate a PowerPoint

01

Sign in with NotePreso

Use the same account as Speaker Notes and Presentation Generator. Check that your account has enough shared AI Credits for your presentation.

02

Choose your PowerPoint

Upload a PPTX up to 100 MB. Older PPT files need the conversion option; conversion can affect animations and embedded objects. Whenever possible, save as PPTX in PowerPoint first.

03

Set your languages

Use automatic source detection or select the source language. Choose the output language, then decide how to handle longer text: preserve font sizes and flag it for review, or try a limited size reduction.

04

Translate, download, review

Confirm processing, then follow the progress on the page. Download your translated PPTX and review report. Open the deck in PowerPoint to check meaning, text fit and layout before sharing.

What gets translated?

Editable slide text and speaker notes are included. Your original upload stays unchanged; you receive a separate PowerPoint. Text inside images is not translated. Complex layouts, right-to-left text, animations and converted PPT files need extra review.

Translation details

How many credits will I use?

Translator uses 1 shared AI Credit per slide, including slide text and speaker notes. A 20-slide deck uses 20 credits. Translation is a separate workflow from creating slides or generating notes. Check your balance before processing.

Can I close the page while it translates?

Processing continues on the server after your job is accepted. Return using the same account and open Recent jobs. If the connection drops during upload, check whether the job was saved before starting again.

What if translation stops?

Read the job status first. A failed job may offer Retry saved job. Reserved credits are returned after a confirmed processing failure where still valid. If a billing check is pending, wait for reconciliation or contact Help with your job ID.

Which files and languages can I use?

The workspace lists the available source and output languages, including regional Chinese and Portuguese options. Files can contain up to 5,000 slides within the 100 MB limit. Translation quality and text fit vary by language and source content.

Where do I manage my subscription?

Open your profile menu to manage your plan, or visit Pricing. Speaker Notes, Presentation Generator and Translator share your NotePreso subscription and credit balance.
