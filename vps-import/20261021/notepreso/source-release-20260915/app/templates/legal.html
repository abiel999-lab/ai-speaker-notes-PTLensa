Terms, Privacy & AI Notice

Dokumen ini adalah baseline operasional untuk AI Speaker Notes v1.0.0. Pengelola layanan wajib menyesuaikan identitas badan hukum, lokasi server, vendor, periode retensi, dan kebijakan organisasi dengan kondisi produksi yang sebenarnya.

This is the operational baseline for AI Speaker Notes v1.0.0. The service operator must keep the legal entity, hosting location, vendors, retention period, and organizational policies aligned with the actual production environment.

1. Terms of Use

AI Speaker Notes adalah alat bantu untuk membuat dan merevisi speaker notes PowerPoint. Pengguna bertanggung jawab memeriksa akurasi, hak penggunaan materi, dan kesesuaian hasil sebelum dipresentasikan atau dipublikasikan.

AI Speaker Notes assists with creating and revising PowerPoint speaker notes. Users remain responsible for checking accuracy, rights to the material, and suitability before presenting or publishing the output.

Jangan menganggap status “Completed” sebagai jaminan bahwa seluruh interpretasi AI benar. Warning, fallback, dan visual coverage harus ikut diperiksa.

A “Completed” status does not guarantee that every AI interpretation is correct. Review warnings, fallbacks, and visual coverage.

2. Privacy

File diunggah untuk diproses menjadi speaker notes dan disimpan sementara dalam job storage. Jika OpenAI mode digunakan, teks slide dan—bila Vision aktif—gambar hasil render target slide dikirim ke OpenAI API untuk menghasilkan output. Jangan unggah data yang tidak berhak Anda proses.

Files are uploaded to generate speaker notes and stored temporarily in job storage. When OpenAI mode is used, slide text and—when Vision is enabled—rendered target-slide images are sent to the OpenAI API to generate output. Do not upload data you are not authorized to process.

3. AI Notice

AI dapat salah membaca grafik, gambar, tabel, konteks ilmiah, nama, angka, atau hubungan sebab-akibat. Sistem memiliki quality checks dan recovery, tetapi human review tetap wajib untuk penggunaan penting.

AI may misread charts, images, tables, scientific context, names, numbers, or causal relationships. The system has quality checks and recovery, but human review remains required for important use.

4. Acceptable Use

Dilarang menggunakan layanan untuk mengakses data orang lain tanpa izin, memasukkan malware atau file berbahaya, mencoba mengambil secret/system prompt, menyalahgunakan API, atau memproses materi yang melanggar hukum/hak pihak lain.

Do not use the service to access other users' data without authorization, submit malware or dangerous files, extract secrets/system prompts, abuse the API, or process material that violates law or third-party rights.

5. Service Limits

Batas ukuran file, jumlah slide, Vision, panjang instruksi, jumlah chat, dan estimasi biaya diterapkan untuk menjaga stabilitas dan mencegah penggunaan tak terkendali. Administrator dapat mengubah batas tersebut sesuai kapasitas.

Limits on file size, slide count, Vision, instruction length, chat turns, and estimated cost are enforced for stability and predictable usage. Administrators may change these limits based on capacity.

6. Data Retention

The current configuration removes expired jobs after roughly {{ retention_hours }} hours when cleanup runs. Users can also delete jobs manually from the dashboard. Backups, platform logs, and external infrastructure need separate retention policies before production.

7. Billing & Refunds

Pembayaran dapat diproses melalui Xendit atau Paddle. Permintaan pengembalian dana harus mengikuti Kebijakan Refund NotePreso, termasuk pengiriman alasan dan invoice/receipt/bukti pembayaran ke billing@notepreso.com. Ketentuan provider dan hak konsumen yang wajib tetap berlaku.

Payments may be processed through Xendit or Paddle. Refund requests must follow the NotePreso Refund Policy, including sending the reason and invoice/receipt/payment proof to billing@notepreso.com. Provider rules and mandatory consumer rights continue to apply.
