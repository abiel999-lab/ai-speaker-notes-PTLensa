FAQ

Questions before you begin

What happens when I try NotePreso without an account?+

You can select a PowerPoint and configure the preview on the homepage. NotePreso does not start processing the file until you sign in or create an account

How much can I try for free?+

Each Free account can process {{ free_weekly_presentations }} PPT files per week, with speaker notes for up to {{ free_preview_slides }} eligible slides per PPT. The quota resets every Monday at 00:00 WIB

What happens after the free preview?+

You can review or download the preview output. Use another free preview while your weekly quota remains; Pro provides full-deck processing according to your available credits

Does NotePreso change my original PowerPoint?+

No. Processing uses a working copy and produces a separate result file. The original upload is not overwritten

Can I still use the full workspace?+

Yes. After signing in, the workspace provides the full generation controls, presentation history and the account plan area
