BILLING FAQ

Clear before checkout.

When does the Free PPT quota reset?+

Free accounts receive {{ pricing.free_weekly_presentations }} PPT previews per week, up to {{ pricing.free_preview_slides }} generated slides each. The quota resets every Monday at 00:00 WIB (Asia/Jakarta). Unused previews do not carry over.

What happens to unused monthly credits?+

The Pro monthly allowance resets to {{ pricing.pro_monthly_slide_credits }} credits when a new paid billing cycle begins. Unused monthly credits do not roll over.

Do top-up credits expire at the monthly reset?+

No. Top-up credits are stored separately and remain on the account. They can be used while the Pro subscription is active.

Which credits are used first?+

NotePreso uses monthly Pro credits first. Top-up credits are used only after the monthly balance is exhausted.

Will I have to upload my presentation again after upgrading?+

No. When you upgrade from a preview in the same workflow, NotePreso keeps the job and can continue the remaining slides after Pro is confirmed.

Where can I request a refund?+

Use the NotePreso Support page and include the transaction or invoice reference. The applicable refund terms are available there as well.
